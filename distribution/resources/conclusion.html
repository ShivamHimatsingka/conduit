Installed

Open Conduit from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar.

The first time you capture an app, macOS asks for Audio Capture permission; the first time you use a microphone, it asks for Microphone permission. Audio never leaves your Mac.

Select Conduit 16ch in System Settings › Sound, or as the input/output device in OBS, Zoom or your DAW.

To remove everything later, run sudo "/Library/Application Support/Conduit/uninstall.sh".
