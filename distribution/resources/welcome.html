Conduit

A free, open-source virtual audio router and mixer for macOS: a 16-channel virtual device, per-app capture, a routing matrix, metering and presets.

This installer adds:

  • Conduit 16ch, a virtual audio device (in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL)
  • Conduit, the menu-bar mixer (in /Applications)

Installing the driver restarts Core Audio once, so audio pauses for a moment. Save your work in audio apps first.
